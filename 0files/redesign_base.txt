  Redesign Plan Overview

  Design System (MoviesAnywhere-inspired)

  - Dark theme with Netflix-red (#E50914) as primary brand
  - Glassmorphism panels with backdrop-filter: blur(20px)
  - 3D card effects - perspective transforms on hover
  (rotateX/rotateY + translateY)
  - Parallax hero with depth layers (translateZ)
  - Inter font + Poppins fallback, 8px spacing system
